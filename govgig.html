When I started on GovGig I asked, How do current day-to-day operations look like?

Stakeholder workshop sessions revealed insights into how contractors use software in practice. I found that…

  1. Customization was necessary to enable companies to tailor the platform to their workflows.
  2. Familiar UX/UI patterns were needed to bridge existing workflows and real-life tools.
  3. Evolving industry needs meant we needed scalable and functional solutions.
